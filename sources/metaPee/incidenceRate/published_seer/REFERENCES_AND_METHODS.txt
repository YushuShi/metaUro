PUBLISHED SEER FIVE-YEAR RISK LOOKUP TABLES — metaPee

Source: NCI SEER Explorer: risk of diagnosis, November 2025 submission; release 2026-04-22; risk period 2021–2023.
Official application: https://seer.cancer.gov/statistics-network/explorer/application.html?data_type=6&graph_type=7
Official source definitions: https://seer.cancer.gov/statistics-network/explorer/cancer-sites.html
Official revision history: https://seer.cancer.gov/statistics-network/explorer/revisions.html
Method: direct published SEER Explorer values, calculated by NCI using DevCan.
These files perform extraction and lookup only. No annual estimation, interpolation,
subtype allocation, derived sex averages, or remaining-lifetime substitutions occur.

Grid: starting ages 30,35,...,80; finite follow-up years 5,10,...,50.
There are 11 data rows, 10 risk columns, a starting_age identifier and header.
Units: percent. Risk strings retain the exact published precision.
Maximum ending age: 90. Cells with starting_age + follow_up_years > 90 remain
blank and requested longer durations must not be silently truncated.
Each supported table contains 74 published values and 36 cutoff blanks.
An unavailable disease or exact stratum remains blank; blank never means zero.
Both Sexes values are direct SEER estimates and are not 50:50 sex averages.
The app and matrices include eight exact published race/ethnicity definitions:
All Races / Ethnicities; Hispanic (any race); Non-Hispanic American Indian /
Alaska Native; Non-Hispanic Asian / Pacific Islander; Non-Hispanic Black;
Non-Hispanic White; Black (includes Hispanic); White (includes Hispanic).
These definitions are distinct: an inclusive group never borrows a non-Hispanic
estimate. Changing Settings selects the reference population used for effect
conversion, estimated cases and prospective sample-size planning. It does not
filter studies by ethnicity or change the population reported in saved studies.
Histology-specific endpoints remain unavailable where SEER has no exact risk.
The default remains All Races / Ethnicities, Both Sexes, age 60 over 10 years.
Offline regeneration: python scripts/build_ethnicity_baselines.py
Byte-for-byte verification: python scripts/build_ethnicity_baselines.py --check
The estimates describe a hypothetical population subject to observed period rates,
not a forecast of an actual cohort's future cancer incidence.

Raw original JSON snapshots and URL/SHA-256 metadata are in sources/.
Each SOURCE_VALUE_RECORDS.csv row records the JSON key, confidence limits,
source bytes hash and source URL. TABLE_MANIFEST.csv records exact disease,
sex and race definitions and unsupported reasons.

Extraction flags: absolute difference >0.02 percentage point OR relative >1%.
Historical flags: absolute difference >0.10 percentage point OR relative >5%.
Every extracted value was compared exactly against the frozen source string;
all extraction differences are zero. DISCREPANCIES.csv compares older vintages.
Vintage and disease-definition differences are documented without claiming they
explain each numerical change. Remaining-lifetime risk is not a finite interval.
