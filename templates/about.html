Evidence and settings

Each collection retains its saved studies, screening decisions, source files, plots, and audit records. Analyses run through the original source-specific engine. The interface does not pool different disease scopes together.

Settings and interactive study selection calculate a view from saved evidence. They do not update the source files. Manual reference risks are specific to each disease; unsupported subtypes do not borrow a parent cancer’s baseline.

Summary plots and workbooks are the original saved exports. Analysis settings apply to the interactive evidence page.
