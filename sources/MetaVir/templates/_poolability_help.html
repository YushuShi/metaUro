Pooling requirements

Pooling requires total sample size, an eligible effect size, and either a confidence interval or a usable p-value. Exact case counts are optional; missing cases are estimated from total sample size using the selected lifetime risk. Exposure unit and scale, adjustment model, subgroup or stratum, and direct exposure measurement may be NA.

A confidence interval may be derived from a usable p-value, or a p-value from the confidence interval. If comparison direction is not explicit, high versus low or yes versus no is assumed and marked Direction imputed. Expand a study to see its imputation reason. Quality, minimum-case, and other selected filters still apply.
