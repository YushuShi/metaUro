INTERPRETATION
Different periods can reflect changes in incidence, screening, diagnosis, and competing mortality. The newer risk period excludes 2020. Race/ethnicity definitions and mortality coverage also differ; broad API/AIAN groups are not paired to non-Hispanic groups. These are documented comparability limitations, not proof of a particular cause for each change. Material changes remain flagged for review. Small baseline values can produce a large relative percentage change despite a small absolute difference.
