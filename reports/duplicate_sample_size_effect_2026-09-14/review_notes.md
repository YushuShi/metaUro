The numerical method is appropriate for finding the requested exact N/effect collisions, with these limits:

- It counts matching rows, including unpooled display evidence. Missing `scientific_pooling_eligible` means unspecified, not false; disease, exposure, quality, and display-only gates must also be considered.
- Original table values and harmonized analysis values are distinct bases. Inversion can give one physical row pair separate numerical-match IDs. Counts across bases, modes, parent exposures and disease scopes must not be described as counts of independent duplicate studies.
- Exact numeric equality misses studies with slightly different rounding, analysis populations, follow-up, comparisons or adjusted results. Conversely, equal N/effect can occur in valid strata or by coincidence.
- Coverage is the saved combined `true_core` views, with `true_all` study arrays verified equal and counted once. It does not cover the alternate ChatGPT-only track, missing/nonpositive N/effect, optional user effect conversions, or formatting-only collisions after display rounding.
