Nutrition & urologic cancer

metaUro lets you explore associations between nutritional exposures and cancer incidence across 14 disease scopes, including bladder, kidney, renal pelvis, prostate, and their supported subtypes.

Choose a disease and exposure to examine the studies, forest plots, effect estimates, and sensitivity analyses. Use the summaries to compare evidence across nutritional exposures.

Analysis settings

Settings control the reference population, age and observation interval, effect-measure display, and sample-size planning. Manual reference risks are saved separately for each disease. A subtype does not inherit its parent cancer’s baseline.

Your settings and study selections update the interactive analysis. Saved summary plots and workbooks retain their original analysis settings.
