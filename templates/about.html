About metaUro: Urologic Cancer & Nutrition

⚠️ CAUTION: AI-Generated Results

This tool includes data extracted from scientific literature with automated methods. Although the evidence is reviewed and released from saved source collections, automated extraction can occasionally misinterpret values, sample sizes, or study designs.

Always verify the extracted data against the original publication before making any clinical or research decisions. This tool is intended for exploratory and educational purposes only.

How It Works

metaUro lets you explore associations between nutritional exposures and cancer incidence across 14 disease scopes, including bladder, kidney, renal pelvis, prostate, and their supported subtypes. Choose a disease and exposure to examine the studies, forest plots, effect estimates, and sensitivity analyses. Use the summaries to compare evidence across nutritional exposures.

  1. Selects a disease scope and nutritional exposure from the released evidence collections.
  2. Shows extracted studies with their screening and quality assessments.
  3. Synthesizes eligible evidence using the selected analysis settings.
  4. Visualizes results with forest, funnel, Baujat, sensitivity, and summary views when available.

Analysis Settings

Settings control the reference population, age and observation interval, effect-measure display, and sample-size planning. Manual reference risks are saved separately for each disease. A subtype does not inherit its parent cancer’s baseline.

Your settings and study selections update the interactive analysis. Saved summary plots and workbooks retain their original analysis settings.
