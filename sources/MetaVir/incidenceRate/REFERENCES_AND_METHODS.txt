CONSTRUCTION
Each *__risk_percent.csv has 61 rows for exact starting ages 30 through 90 and 50 follow-up columns labeled 1 through 50 (years), plus the starting_age identifier column and one header row. Physical CSV shape is 62 lines x 51 fields.
Values are copied from column G, Risk of Being Diagnosed (%), with headers in row 5: Cancer Site, Risk Type, Starting Age, Risk Interval, Sex, Race / Ethnicity, Risk of Being Diagnosed (%), 95% C.I. Low, 95% C.I. High.
Filter exact disease/site, Sex, Race / Ethnicity, Starting Age='Age a', Risk Interval='Next t Years', and Risk Type='Being Diagnosed with Cancer (2019,2021-2022)'. No interpolation, model fitting, annual-rate conversion, multiplication of risks, pooling of sex groups, or allocation from parent-site proportions is performed.
In the source, ages are 0,5,...,80 and finite intervals are in five-year steps, with ending age at most 90. Thus within a supported matrix only ages 30,35,...,80 and durations 5,10,...,50 where age+duration<=90 can be filled: 74 cells of 3050. Ages 81-90 and non-five-year coordinates remain blank. Source suppression/nonnumeric values also remain blank; a published numeric 0 remains 0.
Remaining Life is never substituted for a finite duration, including when age+duration=90. No zero is inserted for missing data. Divide risk_percent by 100 only when downstream software requires a probability (e.g. 3.5462 -> 0.035462).
These are period-based age-conditional probabilities for a hypothetical population alive and free of the specified cancer at the starting age, accounting for competing mortality. A cohort with mixed recruitment ages needs risks matched to those ages; risk at the mean age is not necessarily the cohort-average risk.
SEER's Sex labels are preserved; they are not a measured gender-identity classification. Non-Hispanic strata are not relabeled as Hispanic-inclusive races. AI/AN estimates use PRCDA counties. General respiratory risks are not smoking-specific.
Sources in the workbook: SEER 21 incidence, November 2024 submission (1975-2022); NCHS mortality for the SEER 21 areas for diagnosis risks. DevCan generated the published values. The workbook does not identify its DevCan executable version; none is invented here.

VALIDATION AND DISCREPANCIES
DISCREPANCIES.csv compares 60 overlapping finite-horizon points against official CSR 1975-2017 values (November 2019 submission; 2015-2017 risk period; released 2020-04-15). 48 cross the requested historical threshold.
Definitions: signed_difference_pp = new-old; absolute_difference_pp = abs(new-old); relative_difference_percent = 100*abs(new-old)/abs(old). Units for old/new are percent. Relative change is undefined and left blank when the rounded reference is zero. Historical threshold: >0.10 percentage point OR >5% relative. Same-vintage threshold: >0.02 percentage point OR >1% relative. Equality does not flag.
Historical flags are review signals, not extraction errors. A different vintage is documented but not used to assert an unproven cause or automatically erase a material flag. Same-vintage checks compare exported numbers to the original workbook. They verify extraction, not the accuracy of SEER's underlying model; no independent same-vintage external web table was used.
Every saved matrix will also be checked cell-by-cell by a separate validator reading the XLSX XML directly, independently of the source extraction library. See VALIDATION_RESULTS.json for the actual result.
Additional comparison types distinguish verification of prior local melanoma lifetime values and cross-vintage lifetime comparisons outside the requested lookup grid. Non-equivalent broad API/AIAN race groups have no numerical comparison.
