How to read this diagram

The recorded workflow uses initial screening followed by detailed eligibility assessment and conditional full-text review. The flow is adapted to that workflow; it does not imply that every report underwent full-text assessment.

Inclusion in the review does not mean inclusion in every meta-analysis. A report may contribute multiple results, and pooling also depends on the selected analysis and eligibility rules.
